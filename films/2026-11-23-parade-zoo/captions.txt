The first stars
of America's big
Thanksgiving parade
weren't balloons.
They were real elephants.
November 1924.
A New York
department store
throws a parade
to sell Christmas.
They call it
the Christmas parade
on Thanksgiving morning.
Bands, floats,
and animals on loan
from the Central Park Zoo.
Elephants, camels,
bears, and monkeys.
They growl and roar,
and some children
are terrified.
By 1927,
the animals are gone.
In their place,
giant balloons.
The first one
is a cartoon cat
named Felix.
But when the parade ends,
nobody knows
what to do with them.
So, they let them go.
Balloons drift
over the city,
each with
a return address
and a reward
of up to $100.
This Thursday,
the parade marches again.
Would you chase
a runaway balloon
for $100?
